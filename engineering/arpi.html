A governed management intelligence layer designed to turn fragmented dealership operating data into one traceable analytical model, with consistent KPI definitions and direct evidence behind the numbers managers use to make decisions.

Problem framing

The numbers exist. The definitions do not agree.

Dealership performance data is spread across systems that were built for different operational jobs. The management problem is not a lack of data. It is the absence of one governed definition for the questions leadership needs answered together.

Sales and gross may live in the DMS. Lead activity is in the CRM. Aging and market position sit in inventory tools. F&I performance, marketing spend, and accounting controls come from other systems. When those systems disagree about the meaning of a retail unit, a lead cohort, gross, or inventory status, the management meeting becomes a reconciliation exercise instead of a decision-making process.

ARPI addresses that failure by defining each management question before the metric, assigning the calculation to a governed reporting layer, and preserving drill-through to the transactions or units behind the aggregate.

Design objective

One analytical model, one definition per term, visible reconciliation, and enough traceability that a manager does not have to accept an unexplained aggregate on faith.

System architecture

Keep operational systems authoritative. Add a governed layer above them.

The production design position is intentionally read-only. Existing dealership systems remain systems of record. ARPI adds ingestion, normalization, reconciliation, and management views without writing back into the operational platforms.

Engineering decisions

The project is opinionated about where truth should live.

The most important decisions are less about framework choice and more about preventing multiple analytical surfaces from silently creating different answers to the same management question.

01

Define the question before the metric

Each governed KPI begins with a business question, declared grain, denominator, and explicit inclusion and exclusion rules.

02

Give one layer analytical authority

The SQL reporting layer owns KPI definitions. The web console does not create a second set of formulas.

03

Reconcile rather than assert

Critical measures are re-proved against independent derivations, and failed reconciliation is intended to remain visible.

04

Preserve drill-through

Headline figures are designed to lead back to the deals or units that produced them instead of ending at an unexplained aggregate.

05

Represent unknown states explicitly

Withheld samples, stale exports, unresolved comparisons, and unmet validation gates are states to surface, not blanks to hide.

06

Control scope aggressively

The architecture explicitly excludes production DMS integration, real customer data, real-time streaming, microservices, and AI added only for presentation value.

Tradeoffs and limitations

Credibility requires visible boundaries.

ARPI is designed to demonstrate engineering method. The current repository explicitly documents the places where portfolio evidence ends and production evidence would need to begin.

Synthetic operational data

ARPI does not contain real dealership transactions, customers, or employees. Results describe the synthetic dataset, not the automotive retail industry.

No production DMS or CRM integration

The ingestion design is real, but it has not encountered a live vendor extract, vendor API, credential model, or real source-system inconsistency.

No industry benchmark claims

The project supports comparisons within its synthetic data. It does not label a KPI good or bad against an unsupported dealership benchmark.

Semantic model execution still pending

The Power BI model is committed and statically checked, but source existing is not the same as a Microsoft engine having executed the model.

The web console is not the warehouse

A reachable portfolio application proves the static operating console is deployed. It does not prove the PostgreSQL analytical platform is operating.

ARPI is not an AI product

The project deliberately focuses on governed analytics, reconciliation, traceability, and operational decision support without adding AI for presentation value.

Documented production path

What changes when synthetic evidence meets an authorized dealership environment.

The documented production vision keeps the operating systems authoritative and changes the ingestion source, not the core principle of one governed analytical model.

Portfolio implementation today

  • Seeded synthetic operational data
  • No DMS connector or vendor credential
  • Governed warehouse and reporting definitions
  • Reconciliation against generated sources
  • Operating console reading governed exports

Authorized dealership deployment vision

  • Approved read-only extracts from dealership systems
  • Vendor-specific authorized ingestion paths
  • The same governed analytical model loaded from real extracts
  • Reconciliation against the dealership's own records
  • Operational systems remain authoritative and receive no write-back
